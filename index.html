  3. Block your opponent's threats before they complete their line.
  4. The first player to make five in a row wins. A full board with no five is a draw.

About XO 5-in-a-Row

What is XO 5-in-a-Row?

XO 5-in-a-Row is a browser-based implementation of the classic strategy game played on a 15×15 grid. Two players — X and O — take turns placing their mark on empty intersections. X always moves first. The first player to create five consecutive marks in a row — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally — wins the game. A completely full board with no winner is a draw.

Game Modes

  • Play vs AI — Face the engine at one of 9 difficulty levels. Choose to play as X or O.
  • Local 2 Player — Two people take turns on the same device. No AI involvement.
  • Analysis Board — Freely place stones for both sides, branch variations, and run the engine on any position.
  • Puzzles — Solve engine-verified tactical positions. Every submitted answer is checked by the engine.

AI & Analysis

9 AI Levels

Level 1 plays quickly with limited lookahead. Level 9 searches deeply with full tactical awareness.

Candidate Moves

The top candidate moves are ranked by score and shown in the engine panel.

Forced-Win Detection

Proven forced wins are displayed as M1, M2 … notation, verified by a dedicated tactical solver.

Time Controls

Choose 1s, 5s, 30s, 60s, or ∞ (infinite). Stop the search at any time.

Dual Workers

AI move calculation and position analysis run on separate background threads, so neither blocks the other.

Design Philosophy

The project aims for a clean interface, fast gameplay, and honest evaluation. Scores are always displayed from X's perspective (positive = X is better, negative = O is better). Analysis runs in the background without freezing the page. Controls are accessible by keyboard.

This project is under active development. Features, AI behaviour, and interface details may continue to change as work progresses. Some features may be partially implemented or experimental. Feedback is welcome.
